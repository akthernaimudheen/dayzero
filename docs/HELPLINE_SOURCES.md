## 3. Disclaimers & Traceability Rules

1. **Deterministic Separation of Triage:** Emergency response (112) is separated prominently from mental health crisis counseling (14416) and substance de-addiction consultation (14446 / 14405).
2. **Zero Fabrication:** Neither contact numbers nor GPS coordinates are fabricated. If a facility's exact phone number or OP hours are not explicitly printed on the official departmental portal, they are marked `null` or `Hospital working days (unstated)` and labeled honestly.
3. **External Navigation:** Navigation opens the external device mapping intent (`geo:LAT,LNG?q=Facility+Name`) or OpenStreetMap directly, avoiding proprietary paid tracking.
